Cornell University Unmanned Air Systems (CUAir) is a student-run engineering project team at Cornell University focused on developing a fully autonomous VTOL UAS for search and rescue missions. 

As part of the Structures and Payloads subteam, I am responsible for designing, fabricating, and testing the bay door on our latest aircraft. Our team's UAS is designed to fly at airspeeds up to 100mph, so a bay door is essential in minimizing drag that would otherwise be created by the opening below the payload bay. I designed the bay door with weight requirements driving almost every design decision, and my final design was below the 50g weight limit. 

I used SolidWorks to design the mounts and connectors for the servo and bearing. For the physical door, my final design uses a 0.25" wooden laser cut frame that attaches to the servo and bearing mounts via screws. The area between the frame is filled in using a thin Monokote layer that blocks the wind and adds negligible mass.
