## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/Alex Weng Headshot.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
Hi, my name is Alex Weng! I am currently a Senior at Cornell University studying Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. I'm interested in aerospace structural dynamics, vibrations and dynamic loads analysis, and mechanical design. When I am not working, I spend most of my free time cycling and playing soccer with friends.

During my time as a Loads and Dynamics Engineering Intern with Boeing, I completed dynamic flight load and flutter analyses on B737 MAX planes. More specifically, I developed thresholds to use the plane's CG acceleration to determine if critical loads were hit during severe turbulence events. On top of that, I conducted parameter variation studies on the flutter (coupled aeroelastic vibration) modes of various B737 horizontal stabilizers. 

Additionally, I'm passionate about mechanical design and its ability to shape the future, whether it be in aerospace, clean energy, or some other industry. To me, this process offers the perfect balance between creativity and engineering expertise.
