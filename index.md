  <p>
    Hi, my name is <strong>Alex Weng</strong>! I am currently a Senior at Cornell University
    studying Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. I'm interested in aerospace structural
    dynamics, vibrations and dynamic loads analysis, and mechanical design. When I am not
    working, I spend most of my free time cycling and playing soccer with friends.
  </p>

  <p>
    During my time as a <strong>Loads and Dynamics Engineering Intern with Boeing</strong>,
    I completed dynamic flight load and flutter analyses on 737 MAX aircraft. More specifically,
    I developed thresholds that use the aircraft's CG acceleration to determine whether critical
    loads were reached during severe turbulence events. I also conducted parameter variation
    studies on flutter, or coupled aeroelastic vibration, modes of various 737 horizontal
    stabilizers.
  </p>

  <p>
    Additionally, I'm passionate about <strong>mechanical design</strong> and its ability to
    shape the future, whether in aerospace, clean energy, or another industry. To me, the design
    process offers the perfect balance between creativity and engineering expertise.
  </p>
